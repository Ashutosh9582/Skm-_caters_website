About SKM Caters

Seven decades of trusted hospitality

SKM Caters has been part of family celebrations since 1954. We are known for Rajasthani and multicuisine food cooked fresh for your event, backed by a trained team that manages menu planning, live counters, service and clean-up.

Every event is planned around your guests, your budget and your vision.
