About Us

स्वाद में परंपरा, सेवा में सलीका

SKM Caters is a wedding and event catering service in Delhi NCR, known for Rajasthani and multicuisine food cooked fresh for your event.

Our trained staff handle everything: menu planning, live counters, serving and clean-up, for weddings, engagements, corporate events, birthday parties and more.

Pick dishes from our menu online, or let us suggest a menu for your budget and guest count.
