About Us

Tradition in every taste, grace in every service

SKM Caters has been part of family celebrations since 1954. We are a wedding and event catering service known for Rajasthani and multicuisine food, cooked fresh for your event.

Our trained staff handle everything: menu planning, live counters, serving and clean-up, for weddings, engagements, corporate events, birthday parties and more.

Tell us your guest count and budget, and we will suggest a menu that suits your celebration.
